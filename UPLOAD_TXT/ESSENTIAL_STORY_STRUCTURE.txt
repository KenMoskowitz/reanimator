# ESSENTIAL VIDEO STORY STRUCTURE & SCRIPTWRITING ELEMENTS

---

# SPEED TO VALUE

Get to the core value quickly.

Ideally:

- First sentence
- First few seconds

Remove anything that does not immediately:

- Inform
- Entertain
- Hook

Every line must earn its place.

Ask:

> Can I delete this and still make the point?

If yes, cut it.

---

# CONTRAST

Create tension between:

- What the viewer expects
- What is actually true

Useful structure:

> Most people think X... but the truth is Y.

The sharper the meaningful contrast, the stronger the hook.

Challenge assumptions in a surprising way.

---

# COMPREHENSION CLARITY

Use:

- Simple words
- Short sentences
- Clear logical flow

Aim around a sixth-grade reading level.

Structure ideas wide → narrow:

1. Broad context
2. Specific detail

If a viewer cannot instantly understand where they are in the story, they leave.

---

# CURIOSITY LOOPS

Open a question or tease.

Make the viewer feel they have to stay for the answer.

Do not resolve it immediately.

Build anticipation.

A strong loop creates an itch that finishing the video resolves.

---

# HOOK ALIGNMENT

Align four components:

1. Visual - what is shown
2. Text - on-screen captions
3. Spoken - what is said
4. Audio - music / sound design

Visual and text hooks are especially important because the viewer processes them immediately.

All four should tell the same story.

---

# ATOMIC STORYTELLING

Boil ideas down to their strongest form.

Cut unnecessary sentences.

Condense:

- Five lines → three
- Three lines → two
- Explanation → image

Rewrite until the story is:

- Tight
- Punchy
- Emotionally charged

**Efficiency = retention.**

---

# VIRALITY FORMULA - STORY-DRIVEN VERSION

**Virality = Unique Idea + Large Applicable Audience + Unique Point of View + World-Class Hook + Compelling Story**

Use this as a compass.

The script should check all five boxes where possible.

---

# STORY FILTERS BEFORE WRITING

Ask:

- Will my target viewer care about this?
- Does this solve a real problem or pain point?
- Is this emotionally resonant?
- Would this be shared with a friend?

---

# THE FOUR HORSEMEN OF GREAT STORYTELLING

1. Speed to value
2. Contrast
3. Comprehension clarity
4. Curiosity loops

These are core retention controls.
