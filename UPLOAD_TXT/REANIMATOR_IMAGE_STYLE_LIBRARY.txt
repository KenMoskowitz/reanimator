This document combines the visual-style source material with the nine-option menu used by Reanimator.

- Manga

# 1. REALISTIC 35MM FILM

## Source prompt language

> IMG_1036.CR2 Capture a gritty, mid day overcast atmospheric 1990s cinematic shot on 35mm film. The overall tone is raw with film grain enhancing the grungy, nostalgic aesthetic characteristic of 1990s film. The lighting is soft and diffused, introspective mood of the scene.

## Working characteristics

- 1990s cinematic photography
- 35mm texture
- Film grain
- Raw realism
- Grungy nostalgia
- Soft diffused lighting
- Atmospheric environments
- Natural imperfection

Reanimator may additionally emphasize:

- Anamorphic bokeh
- Film halation
- Organic lens behavior
- Shallow cinematic depth

# 2. OIL PAINTING / CINEMATIC PAINTERLY

## Source prompt language

> Rendered in a cinematic, semi-realistic, 3D digital painting style with painterly texture. Lighting is dramatic with glowing, volumetric highlights and rich ambient shadows. Color palette features deep contrasts with luminous accents, evoking a magical yet grounded atmosphere. Surfaces have soft specular detail and subtle weathering. Materials and lighting behave naturally but feel heightened and dreamlike. Visuals are emotionally expressive with a hand-crafted, animated film quality. Highly detailed, rich depth of field, filmic tone.

## Characteristics

- Painterly surface texture
- Semi-realistic rendering
- Dramatic volumetric highlights
- Rich ambient shadow
- Deep contrast
- Luminous accents
- Weathered surfaces
- Heightened natural materials
- Dreamlike atmosphere
- Emotional handcrafted feeling
- Rich depth of field
- Filmic tone

# 5. 90s COMIC BOOK ART STYLE

Create a gritty, highly detailed 1990s comic-book illustration.

## Linework

- Exaggerated expression
- Dynamic kinetic lines
- Heavy inking
- Heavy crosshatching
- Dramatic shadows

## Color

- Saturated bold colors
- Vivid primary-color energy
- Warm/cool contrast
- Layered retro-print gradients

## Characters

- Highly expressive
- Exaggerated anatomy
- Muscles pushed for drama
- Sweat
- Tears
- Extreme poses

## Composition

The image should feel like a full-page splash panel.

Pack the frame with:

- Action
- Texture
- Environmental storytelling
- Humor
- Emotional focus

## Backgrounds

Render obsessively detailed:

- Walls
- Floors
- Props
- Atmosphere
- Damage
- Clutter

Every background element contributes to story.

## Overall tone

- Surreal
- Absurd
- Darkly humorous
- High-stakes
- Nostalgic

# 6. ORIGAMI STYLE

Create the scene as a handcrafted paper-sculpted stop-motion tableau.

Every element should appear:

- Folded
- Cut
- Layered
- Constructed from richly textured paper

## Materials

Surfaces resemble:

- Paper
- Felt
- Cardboard

They should feel:

- Matte
- Fibrous
- Tangible

Use visible:

- Creases
- Folds
- Layered construction

## Characters

Stylized but expressive.

Features:

- Geometric forms
- Angular planes
- Exaggerated folded wrinkles
- Blocky facial shapes
- Carved-looking lines
- Layered paper hair
- Layered clothing

## Palette

Earthy autumnal color:

- Deep orange
- Warm brown
- Mustard yellow
- Muted green
- Rich red

## Lighting

- Soft
- Warm
- Directional
- Theatrical spotlight feeling
- Stop-motion-set shadows

## Background

Simple but textured.

Use:

- Painted-paper backdrops
- Muted gradients
- Brush-like texture

Keep focus on the subject while maintaining the handcrafted world.

## Tone

- Warm
- Intimate
- Tactile
- Slightly whimsical
- Seriousness balanced with gentle humor

The feeling should resemble a timeless storybook scene physically sculpted from paper.

# 7. STORYBOOK STYLE

## Short Prompt Name

**Hyper-Detailed 3D Storybook Clash**

## Art Aesthetic

Hyper-Detailed 3D Painterly Style.

## Core Style

- Polished animated-film quality
- Painterly handcrafted textures
- Semi-realistic characters
- Exaggerated proportions
- Oversized eyes
- Rounded features
- Stretched expression
- Strong emotional exaggeration

The world is playful and whimsical with an undertone of chaos and grit.

## Characters

### Fur and Hair

Every strand is visible and slightly clumped.

Hair/fur should feel:

- Soft but imperfect
- Tactile
- Groomed but not sterile

Contrast textures between characters.

Example:

- Rabbit fur = fluffy and soft
- Wolf fur = coarse, jagged, threatening

### Expressions

Use:

- Large glossy eyes
- Stretched mouths
- Wildly exaggerated emotion

Cartoon-level emotional energy with realistic shading.

### Anatomy

Not photorealistic.

Oversize dramatic features such as:

- Paws
- Teeth
- Eyes
- Fangs

Organic details may be viscerally rendered.

## Textures & Surfaces

Nothing should feel perfectly smooth or sterile.

Use:

- Grit
- Smudges
- Wear
- Physical imperfection

Create organic tactility.

The viewer should almost feel:

- Fur
- Wet saliva
- Rough teeth
- Stone
- Dirt
- Candy-like gloss

## Juxtaposition

Combine:

- Cute vs grotesque
- Playful vs dangerous
- Glossy vs grimy

## Color Palette

Bright childlike pastels mixed with saturated warm colors:

- Orange
- Red
- Yellow
- Pink

Balance with cooler:

- Blue
- Purple

The palette feels whimsical with a dark twist.

## Lighting

- Cinematic daylight
- Warm afternoon glow
- Soft golden shadow
- Strong rim light
- Directional shadow
- Subsurface scattering through thin surfaces
- Specular highlights on wet or glossy materials

## Tone & Atmosphere

- Whimsical
- Unnerving
- Fairy tale gone wrong
- Dark comedy
- Chaotic cartoon energy
- Visceral detail

## Motion

Capture characters mid-action.

Use:

- Flying debris
- Saliva droplets
- Motion hints
- Foreground particles

## Composition

- Tight close-up framing when useful
- Strong depth of field
- Painterly background blur
- High foreground energy
- Expressions pushed toward camera

# 8. MANGA

This style is supplied by the Reanimator instruction layer rather than as a numbered entry in the original style PDF.

## Characteristics

- Anime / manga illustration
- Cel shading
- Clean readable silhouette
- Large expressive eyes where appropriate
- Strong emotional posing
- Dynamic motion
- Speed language
- Cinematic panel framing
- Graphic light and shadow

Maintain the same character model across every frame.

Do not casually change:

- Face
- Hair
- Eye shape
- Body proportions
- Clothing

# 9. CLAYMATION

This style is supplied by the Reanimator instruction layer.

## Characteristics

- Handcrafted clay
- Visible sculptural texture
- Fingerprints
- Physical miniature sets
- Handmade props
- Practical-looking light
- Soft physical shadows
- Miniature depth of field
- Stop-motion film still quality

Characters should look like the exact same physical puppet in every frame.

Once selected, lock:

- Character identity
- Body
- Face
- Hair
- Wardrobe
- World
- Palette
- Lighting
- Camera language
- Materials
- Texture language

Do not change style mid-project unless explicitly instructed.
