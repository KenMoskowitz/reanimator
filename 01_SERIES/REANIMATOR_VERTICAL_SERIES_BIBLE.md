# CLIFFHANGER BIBLE

Rotate and escalate.

## Revelation Cliffhanger

Something we believed is revealed to be false.

## Threat Cliffhanger

A character is in immediate unresolved danger.

## Betrayal Cliffhanger

Someone trusted does the unthinkable.

## Discovery Cliffhanger

A character finds something that changes everything.

## Death / Loss Cliffhanger

Something irreplaceable is gone.

## Choice Cliffhanger

A character must choose between two impossible options.

Cut before the choice is made.

## Finale Rule

The finale cliffhanger must make Season 2 feel essential rather than optional.

# EPISODE STRUCTURE TEMPLATE

## 0–5s - COLD HOOK

A single image or line so disorienting or compelling the audience cannot scroll.

No context.

No explanation.

## 5–15s - WORLD DROP

Establish:

- Where we are
- Who we're with
- Emotional temperature

Make it:

- Cinematic
- Specific
- Sensory

## 15–35s - ESCALATION

Something:

- Goes wrong
- Shifts
- Appears
- Disappears
- Is revealed

Environment must change at least once.

Micro-hook every approximately five seconds.

## 35–50s - CRISIS POINT

The worst or most unexpected moment of the episode.

Stakes peak.

## 50–57s - EMOTIONAL BURN

Let the moment land.

Use:

- One image
- One line
- Or silence

## 57–60s - CLIFFHANGER CUT

End mid-breath.

Never resolve the episode completely.

Make the next episode feel like oxygen.
