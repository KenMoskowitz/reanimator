# MASTERING VIDEO HOOKS

## The Four Mistakes That Kill Your Views, and How to Fix Them

---

# WHAT DOES A WINNING HOOK LOOK LIKE?

A hook has one job:

**Stop the scroll and get the viewer to stay.**

A strong hook gives the viewer two things.

## 1. Topic Clarity

The viewer immediately understands what the video is about.

## 2. On-Target Curiosity

The subject feels relevant and valuable enough that the viewer cares what happens next.

If either fails, the viewer can leave.

---

# MISTAKE 1 - DELAY

## What It Is

Taking too long to tell the viewer what the video is actually about.

If the subject does not appear until line three or four, the early lines may be delaying clarity.

## Weak Example

> Guys, this is one of the craziest things I've ever seen. You won't believe it when you see it.

The viewer still does not know the topic.

## Better Example

> Here are three simple ways to improve your gut health.

The topic becomes instantly clear.

## Fix

Cut unnecessary opening lines.

Move the topic into the first one or two seconds.

This is **speed to value**.

---

# MISTAKE 2 - CONFUSION

Even if the topic appears quickly, the hook can fail when wording is difficult to process.

## What It Is

The viewer cannot immediately comprehend the point.

## Weak Example Structure

A long sentence combines too many concepts, qualifications, and ideas.

## Better Approach

Reduce the idea to one clean claim.

## Clarity Rules

- Use fewer words.
- Keep enough words to avoid misunderstanding.
- Use simple vocabulary.
- Aim around a sixth-grade reading level.
- Prefer active voice.

Example:

> The dog jumped.

is clearer than:

> The jump of the dog occurred.

## Isolation Test

Read the hook alone.

Ask:

> Is there more than one way to interpret this?

If yes, rewrite.

---

# MISTAKE 3 - IRRELEVANCE

## What It Is

The viewer understands the topic but doubts whether it applies to their situation.

## Weak Framing

> I struggled with skin problems my whole life.

## Viewer-Centered Framing

> If you've struggled with skin problems your whole life, here's how to fix it.

The second version uses:

- You
- Your
- Viewer pain

## Fix

1. Frame around the viewer's pain point.
2. Center the viewer rather than the creator.

Example:

Weak:

> Here are three skincare trends.

Stronger:

> If you struggle with acne, try these three remedies.

---

# MISTAKE 4 - DISINTEREST

A hook can be:

- Clear
- Fast
- Relevant

and still fail because it creates no curiosity.

## What It Is

The hook does not open a curiosity loop.

## Fix

Use contrast.

Create a surprising gap between:

- What the viewer expects
- What the hook introduces

## A / B Model

**A = what the viewer already believes or knows**

**B = surprising alternative**

The gap creates the need to understand how or why.

---

# TWO TYPES OF CONTRAST

## 1. Stated Contrast

Explicitly state both sides.

Example structure:

> Most people use X, but I use Y.

## 2. Implied Contrast

State only the unexpected B.

The viewer supplies A from their existing knowledge.

Example structure:

> This little-known method does X in a completely different way.

---

# WHY HOOKS MATTER

Hooks are treated as the 80/20 of content performance.

Improving the hook can produce disproportionate improvement because the rest of the video does not matter if the audience leaves immediately.

---

# RECAP - THE FOUR HOOK MISTAKES

1. **Delay**: Taking too long to get to the point.
2. **Confusion**: Being unclear or overly complex.
3. **Irrelevance**: Failing to show why the subject matters to the viewer.
4. **Disinterest**: Failing to create enough curiosity to continue.
