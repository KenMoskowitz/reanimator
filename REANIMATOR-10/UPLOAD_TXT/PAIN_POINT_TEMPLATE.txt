# STORY HUG / PAIN POINT TEMPLATE

---

# MARKET'S PAIN POINT

Example source pain point:

> I can't stay consistent with my writing, and I'm tired of doing it alone.

---

# WHY THIS PAIN POINT IS HOT RIGHT NOW

## Post-AI Creative Burnout

Writers can become overwhelmed by tools promising to write for them while still craving:

- Craft
- Identity
- Joy of writing
- Focus
- Momentum

## Loneliness Among Creatives

Free information is abundant, but information does not automatically create belonging.

Writers may want:

- A place that feels like home
- Peers
- Encouragement
- Less destructive critique
- Shared momentum

## Information Overload

There are many storytelling systems.

The audience may not want another authority insisting there is one correct method.

They may want a place to:

- Experiment
- Reflect
- Build confidence
- Continue producing work

## Fear of Failure

A writer may privately ask:

> Am I even any good?

The product should address the emotional problem rather than merely adding more information.

---

# WHAT ARE THEY REALLY PAYING FOR?

The source reframes the offer.

They are not primarily paying for:

- Instruction
- Critique

They are paying for:

**Momentum.**

That means the emotional fuel to continue writing in a safe, smart, supportive environment.

---

# HOW THE GROUP SOLVES IT

Example mechanisms:

- Weekly low-pressure writing prompts
- Encouragement from writers who actually show up
- Progress over perfection
- No judgment about taste, process, or output
- Light-touch guidance
- Reduced feeling of isolation

The offer does not need to promise a publishing deal.

It promises:

- Permission to write again
- Consistency
- Enjoyment
- Community

# REUSABLE PAIN-POINT MODEL

Use this structure:

1. Define the most acute pain.
2. Explain why that pain is intense now.
3. Identify the emotional root cause.
4. Identify what customers are truly paying for.
5. Build mechanisms delivering that emotional result.
6. Clarify what you are not promising.
7. Turn the insight into landing-page copy.
8. Condense into a short description.
