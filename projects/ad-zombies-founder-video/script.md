# KEN BUILDS A BILLION DOLLAR AGENCY FROM A POOL

Project: Personal brand (Path 3). Client: Ken Moskowitz, founder of Ad Zombies. Audio: Ken talking to camera, plus voice over on screen recordings. Runtime: about 2 minutes 20 seconds. Status: draft v2, not locked.

## 00:00 - 00:10 HOOK

Pool. Ken sits at the edge, relaxed, looking straight into the lens.

[ON-SCREEN TEXT: MULTIMILLION DOLLAR BUSINESS TO $1,000 A MONTH.]

KEN: Cancer and AI took my multimillion dollar business down to a thousand dollars a month.

[Beat. A small, real smile.]

KEN: And it was the best thing that ever happened to me.

KEN: If AI is eating your business right now, stay with me. I'm about to show you what a billion dollar, AI-first agency looks like.

## 00:10 - 00:30 THE ENEMY ARRIVES

Cut to Ken, same pool, closer. Quick cuts to your own photos or footage: the New York City event, Gary on stage.

KEN: January, 2023. My company is a multimillion dollar business.

KEN: Then ChatGPT goes public. And by March, we've already lost twenty percent of our monthly recurring revenue.

KEN: My friend Gary Vaynerchuk is speaking at my event in New York City. And Gary tells me one thing. Pivot.

KEN: So I pivot.

## 00:30 - 00:50 ALL IS LOST

Hold on Ken, serious. Cut to hospital or treatment footage if you have it. Sound drops out.

KEN: Two months later, in May, I'm diagnosed with stage three rectal cancer.

KEN: Surgeries. Chemo. Radiation. A whole year.

KEN: Everything goes on hold. I'm not trying to earn a living anymore. I'm trying to live.

KEN: And the company keeps sliding. All through 2023. All through 2024. Until, in 2025, we're at a thousand dollars a month.

KEN: A thousand dollars of recurring revenue, from people who just loved working with us, no matter what.

## 00:50 - 01:10 THE TURN

Back to the pool. Ken leans in, like he is telling you a secret.

KEN: But in those treatment chairs, I had something I'd never had before. Time.

KEN: And one thing to stare at. The thing that was destroying my business.

KEN: So I started sleeping with the enemy. Not the movie plot. The idea.

KEN: They tell you to know your enemy. That's not enough. You have to become your enemy.

KEN: I got close. Real close. I learned how AI thinks, what it's good at, and where it breaks. And I realized the thing killing my company was the thing that could rebuild it.

## 01:10 - 01:50 THE PROOF

Screen recordings, real time, with a visible clock. Ken's voice over the footage. Cut between R1 to R5 (see the recording list).

[R5: THE TEAM. The 32 visible agents listed on screen.]

KEN: Today I run a team of forty AI employees. A creative director. Copywriters. Art directors. Web developers. UX and UI designers.

KEN: Forty of them. Thirty-two you can see right here. They don't take lunch. They don't call in sick. And they don't miss a deadline.

[R1: THE DIRECTOR. You type a plain brief. The creative director hands it to the right department.]

KEN: Here's my creative director taking a brief. Watch it hand the job to the right department.

[R2: THE AD. A branded ad built start to finish, with the real clock visible. Show the fact check and the review verdict.]

KEN: That's a real ad, made in real time. Look at the clock. And before it reaches me, a fact checker and a rules checker go through it. Nothing goes out until I say go.

[R3: THE WEBSITE. A slow, dated site on the left. The rebuilt one on the right. Run a real speed check on camera.]

KEN: This website was slow and stuck in the past. Now watch it come back to life.

[R4: ZACH. A visitor chats on adzombies.com, a lead is routed, a follow-up is drafted and waits in the approvals queue.]

KEN: And this is Zach, on my website. Someone asks a question, leaves their info, and an AI teammate drafts the follow up. Then it waits for me to hit send.

## 01:50 - 02:05 THE BIG PICTURE

Back to the pool. Ken stands, towel over the shoulder or a drink in hand. Wide shot.

KEN: There are already billion dollar agencies. Omnicom is one. But none of them are AI first.

KEN: Mine is on its way to being the first. And the enemy? It works for me now.

## 02:05 - 02:20 THE CLOSE

Ken back at the edge of the pool, close on his face.

KEN: I'm teaching a three day workshop called Build Your AI Team. You'll sit next to me and build yours. Go to workshop dot adzombies dot com.

[ON-SCREEN CALL TO ACTION: WORKSHOP.ADZOMBIES.COM]

KEN: The thing that was destroying my business is the thing that rebuilt it. Biggest blessing of my life.

KEN: Come build your AI team with me.
