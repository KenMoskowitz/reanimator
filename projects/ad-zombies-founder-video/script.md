# KEN BUILDS A BILLION DOLLAR AGENCY FROM A POOL

Project: Personal brand (Path 3). Client: Ken Moskowitz, founder of Ad Zombies. Audio: first person, Ken talking to camera, with voice over on screen recordings. Runtime: about 2 minutes. Status: draft, not locked.

## 00:00 - 00:10 HOOK

Pool. Ken sits at the edge, relaxed, looking straight into the lens.

[ON-SCREEN TEXT: CANCER TOOK MY BUSINESS TO $1,000 A MONTH.]

KEN: Cancer took my multimillion dollar business down to a thousand dollars a month.

[Beat. A small, real smile.]

KEN: And it was the best thing that ever happened to me.

KEN: If AI is eating your business right now, stay with me. I'm about to show you what a billion dollar, AI-first agency looks like.

## 00:10 - 00:30 THE ENEMY ARRIVES

Cut to Ken, same pool, closer. Quick cuts to your own photos or footage: the NYC event, Gary on stage.

KEN: Early 2023. ChatGPT shows up, and my clients start walking out the door.

KEN: By the time I'm hosting my event in New York City, I've already lost twenty percent of my monthly recurring revenue.

KEN: My friend Gary Vaynerchuk is on that stage. And Gary tells me one thing. Pivot.

KEN: So I pivot.

## 00:30 - 00:45 ALL IS LOST

Hold on Ken, serious. Cut to hospital or treatment footage if you have it. Sound drops out.

KEN: Two months later, in May, I'm diagnosed with stage three rectal cancer.

KEN: Surgeries. Chemo. Radiation. A whole year.

KEN: Everything goes on hold. I'm not trying to earn a living anymore. I'm trying to live.

KEN: And the company keeps sliding. Down. Down. Right into the hole.

## 00:45 - 01:05 THE TURN

Back to the pool. Ken leans in, like he is telling you a secret.

KEN: But in that chair, I had something I'd never had before. Time.

KEN: And one thing to stare at. The thing that was wrecking my business.

KEN: You ever see that movie, Sleeping with the Enemy? Julia Roberts? I did my own version of it.

KEN: I didn't run from AI. I got close. Real close. I learned how it thinks, what it's good at, where it breaks.

KEN: And somewhere in that year, I realized the thing killing my company was the thing that could rebuild it.

## 01:05 - 01:40 THE PROOF

Screen recordings, real time, with a visible clock. Ken's voice over the footage. Cut between the shots below.

[SCREEN RECORD 1: a branded ad being made, start to finish. Show the real clock.]

KEN: Today I run a team of forty AI employees. A creative director. A copywriter. Art directors. Web developers. UX and UI designers.

KEN: None of them sleep. None of them ask for a raise. And they don't miss Mondays.

[SCREEN RECORD 2: a website being built, section by section.]

KEN: This is a real brand ad, being made right now. And this is a real website, getting built while we talk.

KEN: Not a demo. Not a mockup. This is how we work every day.

## 01:40 - 01:55 THE BIG PICTURE

Back to the pool. Ken stands, towel over the shoulder or a drink in hand. Wide shot.

KEN: There are already billion dollar agencies. Omnicom is one. But not one of them is AI first.

KEN: Mine's on its way to being the first. And the enemy? It works for me now.

## 01:55 - 02:10 THE CLOSE

Ken back at the edge of the pool, close on his face.

KEN: I'm teaching a three day workshop called Build Your AI Team. You'll sit next to me and build yours.

[ON-SCREEN CALL TO ACTION: [CONFIRM: link, dates, price and what attendees leave with]]

KEN: Cancer took my business. AI gave it back. Biggest blessing of my life.

KEN: Come see why.

[END CARD: Ken "Spanky" Moskowitz | Ad Zombies | adzombies.com]
