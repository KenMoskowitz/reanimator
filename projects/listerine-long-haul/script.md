# PRIYA LANDS AFTER FOURTEEN HOURS

Project: Personal brand / service / client (Path 3). Brand: Listerine. Product: new Listerine Cool Mint (the blue one) 3.2 oz travel size bottle. No call to action (Ken's call). Audio: both (voice over + dialogue). Runtime: 60 seconds. Status: draft, not locked.

## Audience

Travelers on flights of 14 hours or more, about to land. Pain: horrible breath right before they meet someone. Solution: the 3.2 oz travel size, small enough for a carry-on. No call to action.

## 00:00 - 00:10 HOOK

INT. AIRPLANE CABIN, ROW 41, SEAT B - 4:48 A.M. BODY CLOCK, LOS ANGELES TO SYDNEY, HOUR 14

Dim blue cabin light. A hundred people asleep under scratchy blankets. PRIYA (30s, hair flattened on one side, eye mask pushed up on her forehead) sits upright, wide awake. She cups her hand over her mouth, breathes out, and sniffs her palm. Her face falls apart.

PRIYA (V.O.): Eight months. That's how long he's waited for this kiss.

ON-SCREEN: 14 HOURS IN THE AIR. 11 MINUTES TO THE DOORS.

[VISIBLE GOAL] Get through customs and kiss Eli at arrivals, and do not knock him out cold.

PRIYA (V.O.): And my mouth smells like a dumpster behind a tuna restaurant.

Next to her, GARY (60s, neck pillow, mouth open) snores. A half-eaten garlic hummus cup sits on his tray. He lets out one long breath. Priya's eyes water.

SOUND: engine hum, one soft ding. Cut.

## 00:10 - 00:35 FORESHADOW + VALUE DISCOVERY

INT. AIRPLANE LAVATORY - 4:52 A.M.

A closet the size of a phone booth. Flickering light. Priya brushes her teeth with a finger and a paper towel. She chews a mint from her pocket, and the mint is old and fuzzy. She chews a stick of gum, and the gum falls apart into dust.

PRIYA (V.O.): My teeth are wearing tiny sweaters. My tongue is a dry sock.

She holds the faucet water in her hand. A sign above the sink says it is not for drinking. She stares at it.

PRIYA: No. No way.

FLASHBACK (QUICK CUT) - INT. SECURITY LINE, LAX, 6 P.M. YESTERDAY

A TSA officer lifts a full-size blue Listerine bottle out of her bag and drops it into a bin full of confiscated bottles.

OFFICER: Too big for carry-on, ma'am.

PRIYA: It's fine. It's one night. What could happen?

Her mom's voice crackles from her phone in her pocket. A long text slides across the screen, half-read: "Check the zip pocket, honey. Love, Mom."

BACK TO LAVATORY. Priya taps the text away. The captain's voice clicks on.

CAPTAIN (V.O.): Folks, we are starting our descent. Please return to your seats.

ON-SCREEN: 8 MINUTES.

## 00:35 - 00:45 ALL IS LOST + SOLUTION TRIGGER

INT. AIRPLANE CABIN, ROW 41 - 4:58 A.M.

Priya slumps in her seat. Lights come up. Gary wakes and turns toward her to say hello. She jerks back like he is holding a bee.

GARY: Rough flight?

PRIYA (whispering, hand over mouth): I can't breathe at him. I mean at anyone.

She digs through her backpack in a panic. Chargers. A passport. A sock. Her fingers hit the zip pocket and she stops. She pulls out a bottle small enough to hide in one hand. Bright blue Cool Mint Listerine, the label marked 3.2 fl oz. A sticky note is stuck to the side, in her mom's handwriting.

SOUND: one low, clean chime, like a bell in a quiet church.

## 00:45 - 00:55 CLIMAX

INT. AIRPLANE LAVATORY - 5:01 A.M.

Priya twists the cap. Swish. She counts out loud, eyes squeezed shut, cheeks puffed. The mirror shows her face go from tragic, to stunned, to a huge grin.

PRIYA: Oh. OH.

She spits. She breathes into her palm and sniffs it.

PRIYA: I smell like a person again.

She reads the sticky note out loud: "One for you. One for whoever sits next to you." She digs in the zip pocket and finds a second tiny bottle.

INT. AIRPLANE CABIN - 5:03 A.M.

Priya slides the second bottle onto Gary's tray, next to the hummus.

PRIYA: Trust me.

Gary reads the label, shrugs, and smiles.

## 00:55 - 01:00 TRANSFORMATION + CTA

INT. SYDNEY AIRPORT, ARRIVALS HALL - 5:44 A.M.

Sliding doors open. ELI (30s, rumpled, holding a cardboard sign that reads PRIYA with a heart) sees her and drops the sign. They run. She grabs his face and kisses him. He holds it a long second, eyes open, surprised. Behind them, Gary walks past with a wheeled bag and gives her a small thumbs up.

ELI: Wow. Fourteen hours, and you taste like mint.

PRIYA (V.O.): Fourteen hours. Three point two ounces.

ON-SCREEN: LISTERINE COOL MINT. NEW 3.2 OZ TRAVEL SIZE.

Priya turns to the camera, still holding Eli's face, and grins.

PRIYA: Mom was right.

SOUND: arrivals doors chime, crowd noise rising. Cut.
