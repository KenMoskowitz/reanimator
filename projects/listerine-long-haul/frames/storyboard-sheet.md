# STORYBOARD SHEET PROMPT: one 12-panel sheet

IMG_1036.CR2 Capture a gritty, mid day overcast atmospheric 1990s cinematic shot on 35mm film. The overall tone is raw with film grain enhancing the grungy, nostalgic aesthetic characteristic of 1990s film. The lighting is soft and diffused, introspective mood of the scene.

Create ONE storyboard sheet with 12 panels in a grid of 3 columns by 4 rows. Every panel is a vertical 9:16 frame, with thin black gutters and a small white panel number in the corner of each. Same 35mm film grain, anamorphic bokeh, soft halation around lights, handheld feel, and shallow depth in every panel. Cold blue and steel gray palette for the flight. The bright blue Listerine Cool Mint bottle is the cleanest, most saturated color in any frame it appears in. Warm gold only at the arrivals hall.

Locked characters, identical in every panel:
- PRIYA: early 30s South Asian woman, 5'5", medium build, warm brown skin, dark brown eyes, shoulder-length black hair flattened on one side, gray zip hoodie, white tee, black leggings, white sneakers, black backpack, small gold stud earrings, eye mask pushed up on her forehead on the plane.
- GARY: 60s heavy-set white man, gray stubble, thinning gray hair, tan travel vest, blue neck pillow.
- ELI: early 30s tall lanky white man, messy sandy hair, three-day stubble, rumpled green jacket over a gray tee, holds a cardboard sign reading PRIYA with a heart.
- BOTTLE: Listerine Cool Mint, bright blue liquid, 3.2 fl oz travel size, black striped cap, sticky note on the side.

Panels:
1. DISRUPTION. Extreme close-up. Priya in dim blue cabin light, cupping her hand over her mouth, eyes wide with dread. Eye mask on her forehead.
2. DISRUPTION. Medium shot, row 41. Gary asleep with his mouth open, neck pillow crooked, a half-eaten garlic hummus cup on his tray. Priya awake and rigid in the foreground, blurred.
3. CURIOSITY. Close-up of Priya's hand sniffing her own palm, her face collapsing in disgust. A thin orange line of dawn in the window behind her.
4. CURIOSITY. Inside the tiny airplane lavatory, cold flickering fluorescent. Priya scrubbing her teeth with a finger and a paper towel in the steel mirror.
5. ESCALATION. Extreme close-up of a fuzzy old mint on her palm, next to a crumbled stick of gum falling to dust.
6. ESCALATION. Flashback. Bright beige LAX security line. A TSA officer lifts a full-size blue Listerine bottle out of her bag over a bin of confiscated bottles. Priya shrugs.
7. ESCALATION. Priya's phone on the lavatory sink, a text from Mom half visible, a small chime-like glow. Her thumb swipes it away.
8. ESCALATION. Back in seat 41B, cabin lights now on. Gary wakes and turns toward her with a friendly face. Priya jerks back, hand clamped over her mouth.
9. PAYOFF. Priya digs through her black backpack and pulls out the bright blue 3.2 fl oz Listerine Cool Mint bottle with Mom's sticky note on it. The bottle is sharp in the foreground, Priya soft behind it.
10. PAYOFF. Lavatory mirror. Priya swishing with puffed cheeks and eyes squeezed shut, the blue bottle in her hand. Her face breaking into a stunned grin.
11. RESOLUTION. Cabin. Priya sliding a second small blue bottle onto Gary's tray next to the hummus. Gary reading the label, smiling.
12. RESOLUTION. Sydney arrivals hall, warm gold light through tall glass. Priya grabbing Eli's face and kissing him, his PRIYA sign dropped on the floor, Gary walking past with a wheeled bag in the background giving a thumbs up.
