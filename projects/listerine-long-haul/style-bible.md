## Characters

Gray zip hoodie, white tee, black leggings, white sneakers, black backpack. Eye mask pushed up on forehead on the plane. Small gold stud earrings.
- GARY: 60s, white, heavy-set, gray stubble, thinning gray hair, tan travel vest, blue neck pillow.
- ELI: early 30s, white, tall and lanky, messy sandy hair, three-day stubble, rumpled green jacket over a gray tee. Holds a cardboard sign reading PRIYA with a heart.
- THE BOTTLE: Listerine Cool Mint, bright blue liquid, 3.2 fl oz travel size, black striped cap, sticky note on the side.

## Environment
- Airplane cabin: dim blue night light, scratchy gray blankets, row 41, a window with a thin orange line of dawn.
- Lavatory: tiny, cold white fluorescent, flickering, steel sink.
- Security line flashback: LAX, bright, beige, busy, a bin of confiscated bottles.
- Arrivals hall: Sydney, gray dawn through tall glass, warm yellow terminal light, crowd.
- Palette: cold blue and steel gray for the flight, with the blue bottle as the cleanest color in the frame. Warm gold opens up at arrivals.

## Camera language
- Handheld, shallow depth, grainy 35mm. Anamorphic bokeh and soft halation around lights.
- Tight close-ups for panic and breath, medium shots for the cabin, wide only at arrivals.
- Soft diffused light. The daylight overcast in the source language becomes dim cabin light and gray dawn.
- Recurring composition: the blue bottle sharp in the foreground, Priya soft behind it.
